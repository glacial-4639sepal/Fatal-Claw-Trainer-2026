## 🎮 About the Game

Fatal Claw is a 2D hand-drawn Metroidvania action-adventure developed by NDEV GAMES and published by Com2uS Holdings. It released on August 26, 2026, following its Steam Early Access launch on November 18, 2025. Players control Kisha, a resurrected cat exploring a nonlinear underground kingdom, rescuing survivors, absorbing abilities, and uncovering the truth behind the infected creatures.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Fatal Claw**. A single-player trainer for Fatal Claw that provides optional control over combat survivability, movement, ability progression, and upgrade resources. It is designed to complement exploration, mutated abilities, Engraved Skills, Cabitz customization, and the search for the ultimate weapon, Fatal Claw.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed exploration experience, easier access to ability customization, faster progression, or additional freedom when experimenting with combat strategies and movement challenges.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents Kisha from losing health during exploration and combat.
- **Unlimited Healing** — Allows repeated use of available healing actions without consuming the normal healing limit.
- **Unlimited Cabitz** — Provides a continuous supply of Cabitz for adding new abilities and customizing existing ones.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Engraved Skill Use** — Removes normal restrictions on using absorbed Engraved Skills during battles.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Allows Kisha's attacks and abilities to defeat infected enemies with a single successful hit.
- **Freeze Enemy Movement** — Stops affected infected enemies from moving while leaving Kisha free to attack or reposition.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Enhanced Movement** — Improves movement control for rushing, jumping, dashing, and other traversal actions.

### 📌 Progression

- **Ability Unlock Support** — Helps unlock and test mutated abilities without requiring the usual progression through the underground world.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Fatal Claw uses side-scrolling Metroidvania traversal built around running, jumping, rushing, claw attacks, and progressively unlocked abilities. Use Enhanced Movement when exploring difficult platforms, crossing hazardous terrain, or practicing routes through nonlinear areas. Movement assistance does not replace the need to discover abilities that open sealed passages and new sections of the map.

### Tools & Inventory

Manage Kisha's available mutated abilities, absorbed Engraved Skills, healing options, and Cabitz-based upgrades through the game's progression and customization interfaces. Unlimited Cabitz is most useful when testing different ability combinations or developing a preferred build. Make changes carefully and save normally before leaving an upgrade screen.

### World & Environment

The game connects distinctive underground zones through a nonlinear map containing ancient ruins, sealed passages, survivors, infected creatures, and stones that preserve vital force. Explore freely, return to previously visited areas after unlocking new abilities, and use the trainer selectively when you want to focus on story discovery, rescue objectives, or alternate routes.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat combines rushing, jumping, claw strikes, mutated abilities, and timed Engraved Skills. Absorbed skills can be used to counter enemy abilities and create different battle strategies. God Mode and Freeze Enemy Movement are suited to learning enemy patterns, while One-Hit Defeat enables faster clearing of regular encounters. Preserve normal combat when you want to experience the intended challenge of bosses and major battles.
